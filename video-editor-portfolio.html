About me
MC

Crafting stories
one cut at a time

I'm Marcus Cole — a freelance video editor and colorist based in New York, with over a decade of experience turning raw footage into compelling visual narratives. I work with directors, brands, and agencies who care about the difference a great edit makes.

My approach is rooted in rhythm, intention, and restraint. Every cut, every color decision, every moment of silence is a choice. I believe editing is the final rewrite of the script — and I treat it with that level of care.
